## Original library code {#library}

CyAudioCore's original code uses the MIT license. CyDSP is a separate MIT dependency; keep its notices when distributing linked code. Imported device, codec and plugin components retain their own terms rather than inheriting the root library's license.

[CyAudioCore MIT text](doc:license#notice-cyaudiocore) · [CyDSP MIT text](doc:license#notice-cydsp)

## Documentation reader {#reader}

The standalone reader uses [CyDoc 0.1.0 under MIT](doc:license#notice-cydoc). It is separate from the audio runtime. Marked, PrismJS and esbuild participate in documentation generation; their [Marked](doc:license#notice-marked), [PrismJS](doc:license#notice-prismjs) and [esbuild](doc:license#notice-esbuild) notices are retained too. No optional media engine is enabled in this manual.

Ship the complete `Documentation` folder, including `Documentation.html`, `DocAI.md`, `DocData`, scripts, styles and local notices. The CMake installation places it under `share/CyAudioCore/Documentation`. The private authoring workspace is not needed for reading.

## Complete original notices {#notices}

Legal texts are retained in their original language. The same texts are also supplied as files under `DocData/assets/licenses`. Expand a notice to read it.
